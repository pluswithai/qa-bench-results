| Vouchers | vouchers-4 | 3 | Filter field picker: typing a field name and pressing Enter selects nothing but leaves the typed text looking like a chosen field | Real, reproduced independently in two runs. After typing in the "Where" combobox and pressing Enter the menu closes, the field keeps the typed text ("Started" in a screenshot of run 1, identical to the properly selected state in a screenshot; "t"/"d" in screenshots of runs 2 and) while no field is selected: operator and value stay empty and Apply stays disabled. Cause in source: Row.tsx renders macaw DynamicCombobox and only reacts to onChange; macaw's useHighlightedIndex starts at -1 ("Initially we don't show any item as highlighted"), so downshift's Enter selects nothing, and useCombobox never resets the input text when no item was chosen, so the stale text stays even after blur. The field shows a value the filter does not have, and Enter on an exact single match silently does nothing. Low impact (ArrowDown+Enter or a click works), but it is a functional misbehaviour of the control rather than wording/layout, hence 3; a human may prefer 1. | run1-20, run2-15 | [1](https://github.com/saleor/saleor-dashboard/blob/a6e7bee68ab183147ec680e03e86c29f717a11d5/src/components/ConditionalFilter/UI/Row.tsx#L82-L110) [2](https://github.com/saleor/macaw-ui/blob/v1.4.2/src/components/BaseSelect/useHighlightedIndex.ts) [3](https://github.com/saleor/macaw-ui/blob/v1.4.2/src/components/Combobox/Common/useCombobox.tsx#L46-L93) |
